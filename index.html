Wireless communications · Signal processing

Zijun Wang

I study efficient beam training and tracking for large-aperture wireless systems, with an emphasis on near-field propagation, sparse reconstruction, and learning-assisted signal processing.

University at Buffalo, The State University of New York

01

Research

A

Near-field beam management

Low-overhead acquisition, refinement, and tracking for extremely large antenna arrays operating in the radiative near field.

B

Sparse signal recovery

Physics-aware codebooks and sparse reconstruction for multi-user and multipath channels under limited feedback.

C

Learning-assisted wireless systems

Bandit learning and cross-layer methods that adapt beam decisions to uncertainty, mobility, and delay-sensitive traffic.
